# Refund Policy (DRAFT: needs legal review)

- Plans are **prepaid passes** (30 days or 1 year). They never renew automatically, so there is nothing to cancel.
- The price, duration and limits are shown before you pay. A pass starts when payment is confirmed. Buying the same plan again
  extends it; buying a higher plan switches you to it for the full period, with no credit for the unused part of the lower plan.
- **Refunds:** if the service did not work for you during your pass (for example the sites you need could not be downloaded),
  contact [SUPPORT EMAIL] within [14] days of purchase with your account email and payment reference, and we will refund the
  pass unless the service was substantially used. [Counsel: statutory withdrawal rights for digital content differ by country.]
- **Double or mistaken payments:** if you were charged but your plan was not applied (for example you already had a higher plan),
  we hold the payment and refund or credit it on request. This is checked in our admin tools and logged.
- **Late payments:** if you complete a payment after the checkout expired, your plan is still granted.
- **Chargebacks:** please contact us first; a chargeback may suspend the account while it is resolved.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method; crypto refunds go to an address you provide, minus network fees.
