**We do not** sell your data, show third-party ads based on it, or keep a library of the media you download. Access logs hide the
secret part of download links. Log lines refer to downloaded addresses by a short hash, not the address itself.

**Who else sees data**
- **Payment providers** ([PAYSTACK / BTCPAY SERVER / CARD PROCESSOR]) receive what is needed to take payment; their own privacy policies apply.
  Card/M-Pesa data is handled by the provider, not by us. Crypto payments are visible on the public blockchain.
- **The sites you download from** see a request from our servers (or our proxy providers), not from your device.
- **Infrastructure providers** ([HOSTING], [OBJECT STORAGE], [PROXY PROVIDER]) process data on our behalf.
- **SponsorBlock** (optional feature on YouTube links) receives a hash prefix of the video id from our server.
- **Sign-in providers** (GitHub, Google) if you use them.
- Thumbnails are fetched by our server and shown from our own address, so the originating site does not see your browser.
